From raw materials to CoA.

OpenBGA Fresh CoA LIMS is an open-source laboratory information management system for manufacturing quality-control laboratories. It records the sampling, testing, independent review and QC disposition of each material lot, and issues the signed certificate of analysis (CoA).

The code is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License v3.0, and the source and documentation are public on GitHub.

Evidence kept for each lot

Original observation
The result, its units and who recorded it.
Applied requirements
The approved specification and method revisions the lot was tested against.
Independent review
A signature from someone other than the analyst, on the exact revision reviewed.
Issued certificate
A stored PDF. Amendments link to it and leave the original unchanged.

Workflow

Analysts, reviewers and quality staff all work on the same lot record.

  1. Receive and sample

    Receive the lot into quarantine with its supplier, lot numbers, delivery document, quantity and containers, and what the examination on receipt found. Assign its approved specifications and sampling plan. Record which containers were sampled, where, when and by whom.

  2. Test and investigate

    Record numeric, count and qualitative observations, replicates and batch QC, or calculate a result from raw measurements with an approved formula. Each entry names the instruments and reagent lots used. The original result stays on record through any correction or out-of-specification investigation.

  3. Review and decide

    A reviewer who did not record the results signs the review. Quality then checks completeness, failures and open holds before signing the QC disposition.

  4. Issue and preserve

    Issue a versioned certificate of analysis. An amendment states its reason and links to the original certificate, which stays as issued.

Scope and current status

One system serves QC laboratories in pharma and biotech, supplements, food, chemicals and medical devices.

Approved configuration sets the requirements for each segment. Beyond sampling and testing, the system keeps equipment and reagent records, analyst qualifications and stability records.

OpenBGA Fresh CoA LIMS is in development and uses synthetic data. Whoever deploys it remains responsible for intended-use validation and operational acceptance. A QC disposition recorded here is separate from the manufacturer's batch release.
